# KINDLING
*"Did not our heart burn within us, while he talked with us by the way?" — Luke 24:32 (KJV)*

Scripture cards for teens. One card a day, a fire that grows.

---

## PART 1: CARD LAYOUT SPEC

### Design principle
The entire card experience must be consumable in under 30 seconds. No scrolling on the front. One tap to flip. Works fully offline. Content is date-deterministic (same day of month = same card, matching the Daily Wisdom pattern already proven in Genesis).

### Card FRONT
| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Theme tag | Top of card, small caps, single word (e.g., IDENTITY, PRESSURE, GRIT) |
| Verse text | Center, large type, KJV, max ~40 words. This is the hero. |
| Reference | Directly below verse, smaller weight |
| Flip cue | Subtle "tap" affordance at bottom edge |

### Card BACK
| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Real Talk | Two sentences max. Plain teen English, no church jargon. Connects the verse to a real moment in their day: the locker room, the group chat, the test, the mirror. |
| Spark (prayer prompt) | One line, starts with an action word. Something they can pray in 10 seconds. |
| Share button | Renders the card front as an image for texting or posting. This answers the "study with friends" request WONDER users made and never got. |

### Behavior
- **Streak counter:** simple flame icon that grows. No punishment for missing a day, only celebration for showing up. Teens abandon apps that shame them.
- **Offline first:** all 31 cards bundled in the app binary, KJV public domain, zero licensing cost, zero network dependency.
- **Notification:** one per day, user-picked time, worded like a friend ("Today's card is up 🔥"), never like a nag.
- **Card N logic:** card shown = day of month. Months with 28–30 days simply skip the tail cards; they cycle back around next month. Identical to the Genesis Daily Wisdom approach.

## PART 2: THE FIRST 31 CARDS (KJV)

### Day 1 — IDENTITY
**Verse:** "I will praise thee; for I am fearfully and wonderfully made: marvellous are thy works; and that my soul knoweth right well."
**Reference:** Psalm 139:14
**Real Talk:** The mirror and the comment section don't get the final word on you. The One who made you already called the work marvellous.
**Spark:** Thank God for one thing about how He made you, out loud, right now.

### Day 2 — ANXIETY
**Verse:** "Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God."
**Reference:** Philippians 4:6
**Real Talk:** "Careful" here means anxious, and God says you don't have to carry that weight alone. The stuff spinning in your head at midnight has a place to go.
**Spark:** Name the one thing stressing you most and hand it over in a sentence.

### Day 3 — COURAGE
**Verse:** "Be strong and of a good courage; be not afraid, neither be thou dismayed: for the LORD thy God is with thee whithersoever thou goest."
**Reference:** Joshua 1:9
**Real Talk:** Courage isn't the absence of fear, it's moving anyway because you're not moving alone. Wherever today takes you, you don't walk in by yourself.
**Spark:** Ask God for courage for the one thing you've been avoiding.

### Day 4 — FRIENDSHIP
**Verse:** "A friend loveth at all times, and a brother is born for adversity."
**Reference:** Proverbs 17:17
**Real Talk:** Real friends don't disappear when your life gets messy or you stop being fun. Be the friend who shows up on the bad days, and watch who shows up for you.
**Spark:** Pray for one friend by name who's having a rough week.

### Day 5 — PRESSURE
**Verse:** "And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind."
**Reference:** Romans 12:2
**Real Talk:** Fitting in is easy and it costs you everything that makes you different. God's not asking you to blend, He's building something new in you.
**Spark:** Ask God to show you one place you've been copying the crowd.

### Day 6 — APPROVAL
**Verse:** "For do I now persuade men, or God? or do I seek to please men? for if I yet pleased men, I should not be the servant of Christ."
**Reference:** Galatians 1:10
**Real Talk:** Chasing likes is a treadmill that never turns off. One audience matters, and He's already for you.
**Spark:** Tell God you'd rather have His approval than anyone's applause today.

### Day 7 — STRENGTH
**Verse:** "I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me."
**Reference:** Philippians 4:13
**Real Talk:** This verse isn't a slogan for the easy days, Paul wrote it from a prison cell. The strength shows up inside the hard thing, not instead of it.
**Spark:** Ask for strength for the hardest hour of your day before it comes.

### Day 8 — WORDS
**Verse:** "Let no corrupt communication proceed out of your mouth, but that which is good to the use of edifying, that it may minister grace unto the hearers."
**Reference:** Ephesians 4:29
**Real Talk:** Your words either build people or tear them down, there's no neutral. That applies to the group chat too.
**Spark:** Ask God to catch the next tear-down before it leaves your mouth or your thumbs.

### Day 9 — TEMPTATION
**Verse:** "God is faithful, who will not suffer you to be tempted above that ye are able; but will with the temptation also make a way to escape, that ye may be able to bear it."
**Reference:** 1 Corinthians 10:13
**Real Talk:** Every temptation comes with an exit door built in, every single time. Your job isn't to be superhuman, it's to look for the door.
**Spark:** Ask God to make the escape route obvious next time you need it.

### Day 10 — TRUST
**Verse:** "Trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding. In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths."
**Reference:** Proverbs 3:5–6
**Real Talk:** You don't have to have the whole map, college, career, all of it, figured out at sixteen. Trust the One who can see around corners you can't.
**Spark:** Hand God the one decision you keep overthinking.

### Day 11 — FEAR
**Verse:** "For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind."
**Reference:** 2 Timothy 1:7
**Real Talk:** Fear is loud but it's not from home. What God actually put in you is power, love, and a clear head.
**Spark:** Name your loudest fear and remind it who you belong to.

### Day 12 — YOUTH
**Verse:** "Let no man despise thy youth; but be thou an example of the believers, in word, in conversation, in charity, in spirit, in faith, in purity."
**Reference:** 1 Timothy 4:12
**Real Talk:** Nobody gets to write you off because of your age, and you don't get to hide behind it either. You can set the standard right now, not someday.
**Spark:** Ask God where you can lead this week instead of waiting.

### Day 13 — ANGER
**Verse:** "Wherefore, my beloved brethren, let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak, slow to wrath."
**Reference:** James 1:19
**Real Talk:** The comeback feels great for about four seconds and costs you for weeks. Listen first, speak second, blow up basically never.
**Spark:** Ask God for a slow fuse before the next thing that sets you off.

### Day 14 — FORGIVENESS
**Verse:** "And be ye kind one to another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, even as God for Christ's sake hath forgiven you."
**Reference:** Ephesians 4:32
**Real Talk:** Holding a grudge is drinking poison and waiting for the other person to feel it. You forgive because you've been forgiven of more.
**Spark:** Say one name you need to let off the hook, and let go.

### Day 15 — LIGHT
**Verse:** "Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven."
**Reference:** Matthew 5:16
**Real Talk:** You might be the only glimpse of God somebody at your school ever sees. Don't dim it to make the room comfortable.
**Spark:** Ask God to shine through one thing you do today.

### Day 16 — REST
**Verse:** "Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest."
**Reference:** Matthew 11:28
**Real Talk:** Grades, practice, drama, the pressure to be everything at once, Jesus sees all of it. Rest isn't quitting, it's coming home.
**Spark:** Take one deep breath and tell Jesus what's heaviest.

### Day 17 — FUTURE
**Verse:** "For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, saith the LORD, thoughts of peace, and not of evil, to give you an expected end."
**Reference:** Jeremiah 29:11
**Real Talk:** God's plans for you aren't a trap or a punishment, they're peace and a future. The unknown ahead of you is already known to Him.
**Spark:** Thank God that your story already has a good Author.

### Day 18 — EFFORT
**Verse:** "And whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord, and not unto men."
**Reference:** Colossians 3:23
**Real Talk:** The homework, the reps at practice, the shift nobody sees, do it all like God's the coach watching. Half-effort is a habit, and so is excellence.
**Spark:** Pick today's most boring task and do it for Him.

### Day 19 — HEART CHECK
**Verse:** "Let the words of my mouth, and the meditation of my heart, be acceptable in thy sight, O LORD, my strength, and my redeemer."
**Reference:** Psalm 19:14
**Real Talk:** What you think about all day is who you're becoming. Run the inner monologue past God, not just the stuff you say out loud.
**Spark:** Pray this exact verse as your own words.

### Day 20 — LONELINESS
**Verse:** "Fear not, nor be afraid of them: for the LORD thy God, he it is that doth go with thee; he will not fail thee, nor forsake thee."
**Reference:** Deuteronomy 31:6
**Real Talk:** Lonely in a full cafeteria is still lonely, and God sees it. He will not fail you and He will not walk out, that's a promise with no expiration date.
**Spark:** Tell God about the lonely part you don't tell anyone.

### Day 21 — DIRECTION
**Verse:** "Wherewithal shall a young man cleanse his way? by taking heed thereto according to thy word."
**Reference:** Psalm 119:9
**Real Talk:** Staying clean in a messy world isn't about willpower, it's about what you feed on. This card, right now, is you taking heed.
**Spark:** Ask God to make His Word louder than your feed.

### Day 22 — GRATITUDE
**Verse:** "In every thing give thanks: for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you."
**Reference:** 1 Thessalonians 5:18
**Real Talk:** Gratitude isn't pretending everything's fine, it's finding God's fingerprints even on a bad day. Thankfulness rewires how you see everything.
**Spark:** List three things out loud, fast, no repeats from yesterday.

### Day 23 — WISDOM
**Verse:** "If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him."
**Reference:** James 1:5
**Real Talk:** God doesn't roll His eyes when you don't know what to do, He gives wisdom generously and never makes you feel dumb for asking. So ask.
**Spark:** Ask for wisdom on the one thing you googled instead of prayed about.

### Day 24 — PERSEVERANCE
**Verse:** "And let us not be weary in well doing: for in due season we shall reap, if we faint not."
**Reference:** Galatians 6:9
**Real Talk:** The grind feels invisible right now, the practice, the studying, the doing right when nobody claps. Harvest is coming for the ones who don't quit.
**Spark:** Ask God for one more day of not giving up.

### Day 25 — GUARD
**Verse:** "Keep thy heart with all diligence; for out of it are the issues of life."
**Reference:** Proverbs 4:23
**Real Talk:** Everything you watch, scroll, and listen to is either guarding your heart or picking its lock. Be the bouncer at your own door.
**Spark:** Ask God to flag one thing you've been letting in that shouldn't be.

### Day 26 — NEW
**Verse:** "Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new."
**Reference:** 2 Corinthians 5:17
**Real Talk:** Your worst moment is not your name tag anymore. In Christ the old version of you doesn't get a vote.
**Spark:** Thank God for one old thing that's passing away in you.

### Day 27 — LOVE
**Verse:** "By this shall all men know that ye are my disciples, if ye have love one to another."
**Reference:** John 13:35
**Real Talk:** The proof you follow Jesus isn't your playlist or your hoodie, it's how you treat people. Especially the ones who can't do anything for you.
**Spark:** Ask God to point out one person who needs love from you today.

### Day 28 — SERVE
**Verse:** "For even the Son of man came not to be ministered unto, but to minister, and to give his life a ransom for many."
**Reference:** Mark 10:45
**Real Talk:** The King of everything showed up as a servant, so greatness got redefined forever. The strongest move in any room is picking up the towel.
**Spark:** Find one way to serve somebody today without getting credit.

### Day 29 — DOUBT
**Verse:** "And straightway the father of the child cried out, and said with tears, Lord, I believe; help thou mine unbelief."
**Reference:** Mark 9:24
**Real Talk:** Doubt and faith can live in the same sentence, this dad proved it and Jesus answered him anyway. You don't need perfect faith, just honest faith.
**Spark:** Pray this dad's exact prayer with whatever faith you've got.

### Day 30 — LAMP
**Verse:** "Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path."
**Reference:** Psalm 119:105
**Real Talk:** A lamp doesn't light the whole road, just the next step, and that's enough. You don't need the ten-year plan, you need today's step.
**Spark:** Ask God to light just the next step, and take it.

### Day 31 — FIRE
**Verse:** "And they said one to another, Did not our heart burn within us, while he talked with us by the way, and while he opened to us the scriptures?"
**Reference:** Luke 24:32
**Real Talk:** This is the verse this whole app is named for: two guys walking a road, and Scripture set their hearts on fire. Thirty-one days in, is yours starting to burn?
**Spark:** Thank God for the spark, and ask Him to make it a blaze.
